# # Supply Chain & Inventory Analytics Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain, sales, customer, product, and delivery data using SQL to identify operational trends and generate actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on understanding sales performance, product demand, customer behavior, profitability, shipping efficiency, delivery risks, and regional performance.

The project demonstrates SQL skills ranging from basic data exploration to advanced analytical techniques such as CTEs, subqueries, window functions, ranking, moving averages, and time-series analysis.

---

## Business Problem

A supply chain business generates a large volume of order and operational data but needs a structured approach to answer important business questions.

The objective of this project is to use SQL to:

* Monitor sales and order performance
* Identify high-performing products and categories
* Analyze customer segments
* Evaluate regional performance
* Measure shipping and delivery performance
* Identify late-delivery risks
* Analyze product profitability
* Identify high-sales but low-profit products
* Classify products based on their contribution to total sales
* Identify trends and support data-driven decisions

## Business Questions

### Sales Analysis

1. What are the total sales and orders?
2. How are sales changing month by month?
3. Which categories generate the most revenue?
4. Which products generate the highest sales?
5. What is the average order value?

### Customer Analysis

6. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
7. Who are the highest-value customers?
8. Which customers have made repeat purchases?
9. How much revenue is generated by top customers?

### Product Analysis

10. Which products are the best performers?
11. Which products are the most profitable?
12. Which products have high sales but low profit?
13. What percentage of total sales does each category contribute?
14. Which products fall into the A, B, and C categories?

### Supply Chain & Delivery Analysis

15. Which shipping methods are most frequently used?
16. Which shipping methods have the shortest delivery time?
17. Which regions have the highest delivery risk?
18. What is the average delivery delay?
19. Which regions have the highest number of high-risk deliveries?

### Advanced Analysis

20. What are the top 3 products within each category?
21. What is the month-over-month sales growth?
22. What is the cumulative sales over time?
23. What is the three-month moving average?
24. How do regions rank by monthly sales?
25. Which products contribute most to overall revenue?

## Key KPIs

| KPI                   | Description                                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Total Sales           | Overall sales generated                      |
| Total Orders          | Number of unique orders                      |
| Average Order Value   | Average sales value per order                |
| Total Profit          | Overall profit generated                     |
| Profit Margin         | Profit relative to sales                     |
| Delivery Risk Rate    | Percentage of orders with late-delivery risk |
| Average Shipping Time | Average number of shipping days              |
| Repeat Customers      | Customers with multiple orders               |
| Category Contribution | Percentage of total sales by category        |
